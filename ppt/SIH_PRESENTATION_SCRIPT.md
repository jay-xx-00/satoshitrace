Be prepared for these exact questions that SIH judges frequently ask:

### Q1: *"How is SatoshiTrace different from Chainalysis or TRM Labs?"*
> **Answer:** *"Chainalysis and TRM Labs are proprietary, closed-source cloud SaaS platforms that cost over $40,000 per license. Most importantly, Indian police cyber cells cannot legally upload sensitive FIR evidence or seized PCAP files to overseas third-party cloud servers due to data sovereignty and Section 65B admissibility laws. SatoshiTrace is 100% air-gapped, runs entirely on local police hardware, and is purpose-built for Indian UPI mule tracking and Indian judicial standards under BSA 2023."*

### Q2: *"How do you prove that your False Positive Rate is 3.2%?"*
> **Answer:** *"Standard single-model ML approaches flag any sudden large transaction as laundering — which flags legitimate exchange arbitrage and VIP traders. We solve this through our **3-Model Consensus Gate**:
> 1. Unsupervised Isolation Forest (checks volumetric burst anomalies),
> 2. Supervised XGBoost (checks sequential peeling chain graph features),
> 3. Deterministic Heuristic Rules (checks known zero-hop mule and exchange patterns).
> An alert requires a minimum 2-of-3 model agreement before high-priority flagging. In our validation on benchmarked illicit crypto datasets, this consensus requirement dropped false alarms from 26.4% down to 3.2%."*

### Q3: *"How does this comply with Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act / Section 63 BSA 2023?"*
> **Answer:** *"Under the Supreme Court's ruling in *Arjun Panditrao*, electronic evidence must guarantee that the source media was uncorrupted and processed on a secure device with verified custody. SatoshiTrace computes a SHA-256 hash at the exact moment of ingestion. Every intermediate graph partition and consensus score is logged immutably. The exported dossier includes the hardware device signature, processing timestamps, and the statutory certificate template ready for the investigating officer's digital signature."*

### Q4: *"Can your system scale if we feed it millions of transactions?"*
> **Answer:** *"Yes. We decouple ingestion and graph analytics:
> 1. Ingestion performs sub-second batch hashing and SQLite indexing.
> 2. Graph expansion uses indexed adjacency lookups — we only load the k-hop neighborhood of suspect wallets rather than loading the entire 500GB blockchain into RAM at once.
> 3. For enterprise deployment in Phase 2, the graph engine seamlessly transitions to Neo4j or Memgraph with distributed workers."*
